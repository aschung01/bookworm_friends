Four candidate families branch from main — press v: worm-family keeps the retired pre-rename mascot; stack-family speaks the shipping m4 icon's language (offset boards, ribbon notch, fanned stack); line-family is quiet bespoke line art; chalk-family is the icon's mark in its own colours — white chalk mass, green ribbon, hand contour — with the page playing the plate. Three treatments branch further off chalk-family: chalk/dry-line (the contour becomes a breathing dry stroke, the icon's actual hand), chalk/grain-edge (no contour at all — torn silhouette and a speckle band, icon-literal) and chalk/torn-soft (one thin torn contour, the quiet middle). solid-mass branches from main and is the icon's literal read: one bold solid silhouette, no outline anywhere — what the logo actually is at 48px. bedrock-raster and bedrock-ink are the actual AI-generated files from scripts/chalkify_empty_state_art.py — as generated, then with the green plate keyed out. The last three are the route that actually worked, and each one exists to show what the previous one cost: grok-plate is xAI Grok Imagine drawing new illustrations from the app icon as a pure texture reference (no vector of ours seeding it), still on the green plate; grok-cutout regenerates on a keyable white field and cuts to real RGBA, which removes the plate but needs one file per theme — and the two drifted into different drawings; grok-stencil bakes chalk coverage into alpha so one file serves both themes and they cannot drift. The artwork itself

On main: the two shipped SVGs (sadCharacter.svg, smileBookwormIcon.svg) recreated from their actual paths, plus the two Material icons doing illustration duty. The version branches each carry a six-piece set, shown on both themes: worm-family (the pre-rename mascot, redrawn in this set's outline language — note the caveat on its first card: the logo review retired this character), stack-family (the shipping icon's boards, notches and fans), line-family (bespoke glyphs in the register the Material icons occupy today), chalk-family (white chalk masses, ink hand contour, one green detail — the icon's own reading, no SVG filters), and its three treatment refinements — chalk/dry-line, chalk/grain-edge, chalk/torn-soft — which keep the compositions and change only how the edge is drawn, from ruled line toward the icon's dry, grainy mass. solid-mass drops line work entirely: bold solid silhouettes with on-mass details knocked out in surface colour — the icon's own construction, which is why it is the most legible family at spot size. The bedrock pair leaves vector territory: bedrock-raster is generated chalk art (art/gen/*.png), and bedrock-ink keys its plate out so the grain can sit on the app's own page. Both chips show the same file for the raw raster, because raster cannot be themed — that is the route's cost, stated visually. The grok trio is the pipeline that finally matched the icon, and its three cards per piece are worth reading in order: on grok-plate the two chips are one unthemeable file; on grok-cutout they are genuinely two files, which is where you can see the light and dark generations disagree (look at nomatch — a dashed slot on one theme, a solid book on the other); on grok-stencil they are one file again, tinted, with the grain surviving because it now lives in the alpha channel.
