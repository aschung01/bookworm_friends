Three directions × seven states

Shelf books are face-out covers with per-ISBN size jitter (BookWidget). The read pile is brand-coloured spines with rotated titles (BookVertical), as today.

A — Chrome on top

Page title at the top of the screen, floating Shelves / Read switch top-right. Read takes the full canvas as covers by month. Sheets only for Friends, Card, Search.

B — Flighty-faithful

Every page’s header lives inside its sheet with share + avatar. My Library is a sheet too, over a blurred shelf world. Read books become a list at the bottom of the Library Card, like Past Flights.

C — The sheet is the read view

Shelf wall is the world, undimmed. The bottom sheet’s header is “Books read” with share + avatar. Collapsed it’s today’s spine pile; dragged up it becomes covers by month. Sheet position replaces the toggle.
