What should the switch say?

You’re right that “Reading” is wrong — a shelf holds interested and reading books together. Three ways out:

ShelvesRead

Shelves / Read  ★

Stops pretending it’s a status filter. Names the two organizing principles instead: curated by you, versus ordered by time. Survives you ever adding a fourth status.

UnreadRead

Unread / Read

Accurate as a binary and instantly understood. But a book you’re halfway through isn’t really “unread,” and it frames your shelves as a backlog rather than a collection.

WantReadingRead

Want / Reading / Read

Mirrors status 0/1/2 exactly, and matches the badges you already ship. But it splits every shelf in half — a shelf mixing both is the point of shelves — and it’s wide for a floating control.
