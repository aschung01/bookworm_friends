Library Card — sharing

The Library Card and its share

Direction decided; not yet built. Phase 3 shipped the Card and a share that hands a PNG straight to the platform; this page draws what goes between the two, and what the image has to carry if it is to bring anyone back. Read Today, for reference first — the third screen there is the whole problem. The reasoning behind what already ships is in docs/superpowers/plans/2026-08-16-phase-3-library-card-plan.md; the shell it sits in is drawn in docs/mockups/library-shell/decided.html.

Settled in review: the export carries Candlelight, so what you press is what you send. The share is the image and nothing else — no link in the payload, no hosted copy, no landing page — which is what Flighty does too. The return path is printed on the card instead (el-mrz), because a caption does not survive a screenshot and pixels do. Identity is a new handle, and the reason is narrow: a machine-readable zone is Latin-only, and 142 of the 153 display names are not. The name still shows in the record, in its own script; the handle goes in the strip. username keeps its job otherwise — poke and search are unchanged, because nothing here needs a URL. main ships; tile-share is parked.

Sourced from the real code. Tile fills are computed rather than eyeballed, which the shell drawings did not do: brandFill #067657 to Color.lerp(brandFill, black, .22) = #055C44 for the hero (statTileHeroFill), and surfaceVariant #E9ECEF to Color.lerp(surfaceVariant, primaryText, .07) = #DBDEE1 for the rest (statTileFill) — both grading darker, with hero text at white 88% and tile text at primaryText 70%, the two values library_card_contrast_test.dart forced. The exported image is kShareableCardSize 360×450 at the 3× capture, i.e. 1080×1350. The sheet is drawn at the medium detent, which is where the Card tab opens (initialDetent: LibrarySheetDetent.medium), and the expanded header is already up there, so share needs no drag. The card stock and the candlelight palette are proposals, not tokens — app_theme.dart has no cream and no flame, and the artifact is deliberately pinned to one look in both themes. Scope: the share that starts on the Card tab. The library bar has a share button of its own and it is left alone here — that entry point belongs to My Library and is going to work differently. Nothing is open: the last one, the migration sequencing on el-mrz, dissolved once the audit established there is no client in the wild to break. Filter with /.
