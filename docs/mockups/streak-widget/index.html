Matrix

Five things to set. The grid is hour × background — a day passing down the page, ten candidate grounds across it. Voice, type and the unlit core are selectors now because they are decided; the ground is the grid because it is not. Under the number is the newest question and a selector rather than a second grid, because two open axes at once stop being a comparison.

Recorded carries no line, at any hour, under any ground: the line's job is to ask for something, and once the night is in there is nothing to ask for. It is the top row, one tile per ground — the inversion is the comparison between that tile and the hours below it. The ground inverts to carry that state — darkening toward midnight while the day is open, pale the moment it is recorded — which moves the state off a single glyph, where dark mode had the two tints only 1.5:1 apart. Grounds are interpolated between anchors that are real card_lighting.dart and AppColors values; the two ramps that invent say so in their name and in the legend. Type is the real Pretendard ExtraBold and GowunBatang Bold from assets/fonts/, not a stand-in.
