Streak widget · as it ships

Every state the tile can be in, in both locales, at the real 158×158 and 338×158. Nothing here is retyped. The copy is read out of app_en.arb / app_ko.arb, the ground ramp and the poses out of StreakWidget.swift, the tier hours out of StreakSnapshot.swift, the cut-outs are the extension's own Assets.xcassets at 3x, and the flame is printed by rive/streak_flame/icon.py --svg. Rebuild with scripts/render_final_widget.py.

What this page is not: a rendering of SwiftUI. It is the same numbers drawn by a browser, so it settles copy, colour, pose and crop — and cannot settle what lineLimit(3), minimumScaleFactor or the sticker numeral's twelve offset copies actually do on a device. Those need a simulator.

It also cannot see anything WidgetKit or CoreText does around the tile, and two defects have already got through that gap — both found by measuring a simulator screenshot against this page with scripts/measure_widget_shot.py, and neither visible as an error anywhere. The 14pt inset is drawn here directly, where on iOS 17 the system adds its own ~16pt content margins unless the widget calls contentMarginsDisabled(). And the numeral is set line-height: 1 here, where SwiftUI uses Nunito's natural 1.364em line box — which put the run row 7.3pt lower on device, measured 23.0pt from the tile's edge against the 15.5pt this page draws. A tile with either defect renders as a smaller, more timid version of the design rather than as anything broken.

systemMedium — the family that kept the book

Book bottom-left, copy top-right, which leaves the run in the corner the eye lands on and the cat the one opposite it. Small dropped the jacket, and that is what bought it a line at all; medium keeps the jacket, title, author and progress, and puts them along the floor so they read as one object rather than as a sidebar. The copy gets 133pt here against the two-column version's 76pt — 1.74×, and wider than on small, which the layout it replaced was not. Copy is right-aligned because it hugs the right edge; flipping it to leading is one line.

What it costs is that the cat comes down from 70% of the tile to 62% to stay out of the copy's corner, so the character is smaller here than in any other tile. The run row is small's, at 28 and 40 — there is no jacket beside it to make room for any more. The recorded tile leaves the top-right corner empty, because recorded has no line in any locale.

The bookmark is the library's own ribbon, at the tile's scale, slid in from the fore-edge by how far through the book the reader is — the same track the shelf and the Library Card use, so the mark reads the same position at three sizes. Drawn here at 41%, matching the progress bar beside it.

The same two with a pale jacket, #FDFDFB — what the app actually wrote for a Korean paperback whose cover is white paper. cover_color is sampled from the real jacket, so this is a common case and not an edge, and on the recorded tile's cream ground an unbordered rectangle of it is invisible. The 1pt edge is the whole fix; the page drew only the brown above until the device showed otherwise.

These two also carry no recorded position, which is the other end of the bookmark's track: a null progress pins the ribbon at the fore-edge rather than sending it to the gutter, because every book has a null position until someone answers the percent wheel and a mark at the gutter would claim the reader had barely started. Note the ribbon draws here while the progress bar does not — a bar at zero is a claim, the ribbon at its pin is the absence of one.

systemMedium — the two-column version, which lost

Kept rather than deleted, because it is the arrangement the four-corner one is an argument against. The jacket is a column of its own at the far left with the run, title, author, progress and line stacked beside it, and it cost twice over. The line inherited small's 52% of the column cap — but the column starts a jacket and a gap in from the left, so the copy came out 76pt wide, narrower on the wide tile than on the narrow one. And a jacket at the left margin with everything else to its right reads as a sidebar rather than as part of the tile.
