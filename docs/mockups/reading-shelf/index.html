Libstack — the reading shelf

A shelf for the books you have open

Today a book at bookStatusReading stays on the shelf it belongs to and is promoted to the head of that row by withReadingFirst. The proposal is to take it off the shelf entirely and stand it on one shelf of its own at the top of the library — the same move withoutFinishedBooks already makes for a book that has been read, which leaves the shelves for the read pile at the bottom.

Two things are decided and every drawing assumes them. Shelves are a queue, so a book leaving its shelf when it is opened is correct rather than lossy, and a shelf ends up meaning “books I have not started”. And the reading shelf is not a drop target — status is changed from the book's own sheet, never by dragging — which is what the edit-mode screen is about, because it is the one place that decision is visible.

What the drawings turned up. Four things that are not obvious from the prose, flagged inline in red where they occur: the hero flight from a reading cover has no partner to fly to; “Reading” collides with a shelf a reader may already have named that; the reading shelf can overflow its own row like any other; and in edit mode it is the one shelf that does nothing.

And seven treatments redraw the shelf itself — books lying flat on the plank, one left face-down mid-chapter, a tinted alcove, a typographic ledge in the app's serif, a now-reading card, a leaning hero, and D+n day stamps from start_date. Each is priced in points and annotated with what it gives up; t-nook composes with nearly all of the others, and t-band is the one deliberate idiom-breaker.

Then four hybrids compose ledge × lean × days, and four label scales ask how small the word can get — 1.5× title, the title token itself, subtitle in sans, and a caption eyebrow over a full-width row. All four hold the same three books, so the only variable is how much library the word costs you.

The lean had a bug the drawing caught. With transform-origin: 90% 100% the counter-clockwise rotation drove the cover's lower-left corner below the plank — the book pierced the shelf instead of standing on it. It now pivots on its bottom-left corner, the one still touching the board, which makes that corner the lowest point of the transformed box at any angle.

Colours are AppColors.light verbatim (surface #FFFFFF, surfaceVariant #E9ECEF, brandText #067657); type is the eight tokens from app_text_styles.dart at 0.615; the plank is 8pt of surface with a 0 2 2 shadow at 25%, per shelf_widget.dart; the bookmark is bookmarkIcon.svg's own geometry — a white notched ribbon, 13.5×30 with the notch at 80.6%, hung 12.5 in from the cover's right edge. Only covers and spines exist as densities; leaning was withdrawn.
