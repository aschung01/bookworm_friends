The options

Start here. Eight treatments for a cover with no thumbnail, each drawn at all three widths the app uses (78pt in the read grid, 84pt on a shelf, 115pt on the details page) plus one at 2× so the artwork itself can be judged. Options that put type on the swatch also show the full palette, because that is where contrast fails and where the mascot collides. Contrast figures are WCAG 2.1, computed against a function checked against the three ratios app_theme.dart states for itself. Option 1 is what ships today. The title is left-aligned and top-anchored in every one of them, and none carries a corner mark — a colophon is something a real cover earns.
