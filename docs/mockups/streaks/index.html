Reading streaks — the bookmark, the card, the list

Correction, found after these were drawn — two of them, and both change the design.

1. The app already has a bookmark, and it is white. ReadingBookmark in lib/ui/widgets/book/reading_bookmark.dart ships today: bookmarkIcon.svg, a 22×38 box holding a 13.5×30 white notched ribbon, inset kReadingBookmarkInset 8 from the cover's right edge, its shadow dropped 4 and blurred 4 at half strength. It is drawn on shelf rows and on the Library Card, and it already means something: this is the book you have open. Every ribbon below is an invented brand-green one, 18pt wide, 74pt across the cover — precisely the mistake pitfalls.md records as having cost the library set three review rounds. These need redrawing against the real asset, and the design question changes with them: may the mark that means open also mean page 148, or is that two jobs for one object? Its doc comment is worth reading first — it was two marks once, and was deliberately made one.

2. page_count is 35% covered, and structurally so. 20260816160000_book_page_count.sql measured that on the migrated corpus, and backfill_book_page_count.py says why it will not improve: Kakao, the only provider that actually knows this corpus, has no page field at all — eleven keys, none of them pages — and Google Books found 16 of 40 ISBNs where Kakao found 40 of 40. So about two books in three have no track to drag along. That is the inversion the nocount screen warns about, now confirmed: the fallback button is the majority path, not the edge. The ribbon can only be the feature if the reader is asked for the count once when starting a book — which is a real option rather than a consolation, since they are holding the object and the number is printed inside it.

Nothing here is built and nothing is decided. A streak needs a daily signal and this app has none: the schema records books, memos, praise and pokes, but no row anywhere means “I read today”. These drawings propose that the signal is a bookmark you move, so one gesture feeds the streak, the progress percentage, a real reading span and the dormant activity feed at once. Drawn at 1px = 1pt on a 393pt frame, cropped to the book-details hero, the same unit the praise-region set uses. Colours are AppColors.light verbatim — surfaceVariant #E9ECEF for the hero, brandFill #067657 for the ribbon body and the page tag, brand #09BC8A for its lit edge only, because app_theme.dart documents that token as decoration at 2.45:1 and the tag carries text. The cover is 120×180 from kDefaultCoverAspect 2/3, the shelf 8pt with offset(0,2) blur 2 black@25%, the badge h10/v4 radius 10. The three versions are the three things a ribbon can be attached to, and the consequence is resolution: 2.5pt of travel per page hanging from the chrome, 0.56 on the cover, 0.33 across the fore-edge — all computed in SECTION 2, so no drawing can disagree with the figure.
