Shelves that overflow their row

A shelf is one horizontal ListView, and on a 390pt phone only 3.4 books fit in it — bookHeight is screenHeight * 0.15 (126.6), the cover is 2/3 of that (84.4 wide), the row is screenWidth * 0.95 less 15pt of padding each side, and the separator is 15. So a twelve-book shelf shows three and a sliver, and the only thing saying otherwise is a clip through the fourth cover. Three options are drawn below.

Colours are AppColors.light verbatim (surface #FFFFFF, surfaceVariant #E9ECEF, brandText #067657); type is the eight tokens from app_text_styles.dart at 0.615; the plank is 8pt of surface with a 0 2 2 shadow at 25%, per shelf_widget.dart.
